Madonna Louise Ciccone is an American singer, songwriter, record producer, and actress. Dubbed the "Queen of Pop", she is known for her continual reinvention and versatility in music production, songwriting, and visual presentation. Her works explored social, political, sexual, and religious themes, generating both controversy and critical acclaim. Regarded as one of the most influential musicians of all time, Madonna has had a significant socio-cultural impact across her career and broke gender barriers in popular music.
