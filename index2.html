Star's most iconic albums

Facts about Madonna's best and most famouse albums


Do you know what song gave Madonna her career? Or do you know which of her albums had every song go numer 1 on Bilboard. Find that out and many new information about your favorite albums.


Madonna (1983)

Her self-titled debut album featured breakthrough dance hits like "Holiday" and "Lucky Star".

Like a Virgin (1984)

The album that solidified her as a global superstar, topping the Billboard 200 charts.

True Blue (1986)

Dedicated to her then-husband Sean Penn, featuring "Papa Don't Preach" and "La Isla Bonita".

Like a Prayer (1989)

An acclaimed masterpiece dealing with religion and family, causing massive cultural waves.

Erotica (1992)

A bold, experimental concept album released alongside her controversial "Sex" coffee table book.

Bedtime Stories (1994)

A smoother R&B-influenced sound featuring the chart-topping, hypnotic track "Take a Bow".

Ray of Light (1998)

A brilliant electronic and techno transformation that won multiple Grammy Awards.

Music (2000)

Blending country music accents with electronic dance beats to create a unique pop flavor.

Confessions on a Dance Floor (2005)

A continuous non-stop club mix album that sampled ABBA and won a Grammy for Best Electronic Album.
