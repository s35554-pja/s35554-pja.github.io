MADONNA'S DISCOGRAPHY

Star's most iconic albums

Facts about Madonna's best and most famous albums


Do you know what song gave Madonna her career? Or do you know which of her albums had every song go numer 1 on Billboard. Find that out and many new information about your favorite albums.


Like a Virgin (1984)

The album that solidified her as a global superstar, starting her career with hits like "Like a Virgin" and "Material Girl"

True Blue (1986)

Dedicated to her then-husband Sean Penn, featuring "Papa Don't Preach" and "La Isla Bonita". All singles from the album hit the number 1 spot in US and UK.

Like a Prayer (1989)

An acclaimed masterpiece dealing with religion and family, causing massive cultural waves.

Erotica (1992)

A bold, experimental concept album released alongside her controversial "Sex" coffee table book.

Ray of Light (1998)

Album influenced by techno and rave sound as well as Buddhism. Touching on topics such as spirituality and relations.

Confessions on the Dance Floor (2005)

Disco-dance resurrection with songs that are perfect for the dance floor and famous ABBA sample.
